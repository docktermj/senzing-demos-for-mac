# Security and Compliance Notes

- **Data origin:** all three sources are synthetic and generated by the bootcamp. They contain invented
  names, emails, phone numbers and addresses and describe no real person.
- **Identifiers:** every email and phone number belongs to exactly one invented customer.
- **Version control:** `data/raw/*` is excluded in `.gitignore`. Anything you replace this data with
  (your own exports) may contain personal information and should stay out of version control too.
- **If you use your own data later:** confirm you have permission to use it, and consider anonymizing
  personal information for testing.
