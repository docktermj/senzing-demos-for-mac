Data quality assessment

Source-level scores and record-type field coverage for the three collected datasets.

Per-field completeness

Each bar is the percentage of records of that type with a non-empty value for the field. Only fields observed at least once for that type are shown; record keys and record type are omitted from this field-level view.

Overall completeness is the mean per-record share of applicable source fields, with applicability checked by record type and identifier attributes grouped together. These bars show individual field coverage; they are diagnostic, not a substitute for that calculation.
Coverage was measured locally from the registered JSONL files. No record-level values are included in this report.
